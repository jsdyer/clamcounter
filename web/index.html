What these prices mean

Menu evidence, not live stock

Each entry links to a restaurant-owned menu or its linked ordering page. Prices may differ at the counter. Market prices stay unknown. “Fresh clams” alone does not tell us whole clams versus strips.

Fair comparisons

We keep published portion descriptions and side dishes as stated. No cost per gram is invented for an unweighed dish. Restaurant hours, where recorded, are regular listed hours—not a booking or holiday-hours check.

The optional estimate uses Nova Scotia’s 14% HST. Final till rounding and tax treatment may differ.

Your shortlist stays here

Searches, favourites and personal quotes are not sent to restaurant sites. Links open the original site only when selected. CoolCode records aggregate page visits; it does not receive your comparison. Use Save or Export deliberately; browser storage can be cleared.
